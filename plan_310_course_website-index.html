How Grades Work (Ungrading)

Phase Process
Beginning of Semester Set your own goals and intentions for what you’d like to get out of this class.
Mid-Semester Self-assess progress toward goals, course assignments, and objectives; make adjustments as needed.
End of Semester Write a final reflection considering progress made and what was gained.
Final Grade Based on the grade you give yourself in the final assessment. The instructor reserves the right to raise the grade, or will discuss with you before lowering it based on engagement.

Policies

Communication

Contact the instructor in-person or via email. Expect a response within 48 hours. Emails are not regularly checked between 7pm-7am or on weekends. Use "URGENT" in the subject line for time-sensitive matters.

Attendance

Enrolled students who never attend will be flagged for non-participation and administratively dropped before the tuition refund deadline.

Incomplete Grades

Given only for compelling reasons, consistent with UH Mānoa Undergraduate Division policies.

Classroom Technology

Technology is allowed if non-disruptive to learning. The instructor may change this policy if necessary.

Academic Integrity & AI

All submitted work must be your own. Use appropriate citations. Submitting AI-generated work as your own is plagiarism. AI may be used for brainstorming, but its use must be acknowledged in the references section.

Practice

Test your knowledge with the Practice quiz.
